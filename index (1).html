Lab 3 · Think critically

Does association prove causation?

Consider other differences between the groups. People with high UPF consumption may also differ in age, body mass index, physical activity, or socioeconomic circumstances. How might these factors affect the observed association?

Important: A cohort study establishes the temporal sequence between a measured exposure and subsequent new cases, but observational associations alone do not demonstrate causation.

Core takeaway

Start with disease-free participants → classify exposure → follow participants forward → measure incident disease → compare risk in exposed and unexposed participants.
